### BCGT-000238: Stability of mining counterstrategies
- **Subfield:** Selfish mining, withholding strategies and attack rationality
- **Open:** Characterize repeated-game outcomes when pools retaliate against withholding, identifying when retaliation restores honesty or concentrates effective power.
- **Status:** Research target strengthened by 2025 counterstrategy results showing that sufficiently large pools can exploit another deviator.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Punishing attackers can itself weaken decentralization.
- **Key works:** Gal, Szabo and Rottenstreich (2025), *Majority is not Needed: A Counterstrategy to Selfish Mining*, DOI:10.1109/ICBC64466.2025.11114470; Kiayias et al. (2016), *Blockchain Mining Games*, arXiv:1607.02420.
